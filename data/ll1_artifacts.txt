VP_TAIL -> VERB VP_TAIL | NP VP_TAIL | PP VP_TAIL | ADV VP_TAIL | ε
PP -> PREP NP
ADV -> ADVWORD ADV_TAIL
ADV_TAIL -> ADVWORD ADV_TAIL | ε
