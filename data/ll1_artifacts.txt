CONTEXT-FREE GRAMMAR
======================================================================
ADV -> ADVWORD ADV_TAIL
ADV_TAIL -> ADVWORD ADV_TAIL | ε
PP -> PREP NP
S -> CLAUSE S_TAIL
S_TAIL -> CONJ CLAUSE S_TAIL | CLAUSE S_TAIL | ε
CLAUSE -> VOCATIVE CLAUSE | CORE
VOCATIVE -> NOUN | DET NOUN
CORE -> VP | NP CORE_TAIL | ADV | PP
CORE_TAIL -> VP | ADV CORE_TAIL | PP CORE_TAIL | ε
NP -> DET NP_HEAD | NEG NP_HEAD | NP_HEAD
NP_HEAD -> ADJ NP_TAIL | NOUN NP_TAIL | PRON | NUM
NP_TAIL -> NOUN NP_TAIL | ADJ NP_TAIL | PP NP_TAIL | ε
VP -> NEG AUX VP_TAIL | NEG VERB VP_TAIL | AUX VERB VP_TAIL | VERB VP_TAIL
VP_TAIL -> VERB VP_TAIL | NP VP_TAIL | PP VP_TAIL | ADV VP_TAIL | ε
